# Demo script: your screen-recording parts

This is what **you** say and click between Scholar's clips. Your 6 demo parts add up to about **2 minutes 45 seconds**, so with Scholar's clips (about 2:15) the video lands near 5 minutes.

Each part is short, so record them **one at a time**. A mistake only means redoing 30 seconds. Times are rough, and it's fine to be a little over or under.

---

## Before you record

- **Use the demo student.** On the home page, click **Try the demo**, or use **Me → Demo student** if you're logged in. It already has a built Nest, chicks, corn, and a calendar, so nothing looks empty. Building a new plan takes a minute, so don't do it live.
- **Chrome, full screen** (Cmd + Ctrl + F). Close other tabs and turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications pop up.
- **Zoom the page to about 110–125%** (Cmd + plus) so the text is readable in the video.
- **Move the mouse slowly** and pause a moment after each click. Viewers need time to see what happened.
- Prepare one homework question for the Coop, like **"How do I find the vertex of y = x² − 4x + 3?"**

## Part 2 · Nest Builder (~40 sec)
*Plays after Scholar's "Nest Builder" clip.*

**Show:** open **Nest**, then briefly open the "new nest" form and point at the fields (class, test date, current and goal grade, notes or study guide upload). Don't submit. Then open the demo's existing nest and scroll the egg path. Tap the wiggling egg to show a lesson, then the quiz.

> "The Nest Builder is the heart of Cluck In. You tell it your class, your test date, your current grade, and your goal grade. You can also paste notes or upload your study guide, so the plan matches what *your* class actually covers.
>
> It works backwards from your goal and builds a day-by-day path. Each egg is a short lesson, taught your way, followed by a five-question quiz. Get it all right and the egg hatches into a chick. Every few levels there's a corn chest, and at the end, a mock exam predicts your grade."

## Part 4 · Egg Timer (~30 sec)
*Plays after Scholar's "Egg Timer" clip.*

**Show:** open **Timer**, click a preset (Classic 25/5), and press **Start focus**. Let it run a few seconds, then point at the countdown in the browser tab. Scroll down to the coop, the yard, and the fried eggs and drumsticks.

> "The Egg Timer helps you actually focus. Pick a session, like 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break, and your chick keeps an egg warm while you study. It keeps running even if you switch tabs.
>
> Finish the session and the egg hatches into a new chick for your flock. Quit early, and it gets fried. And if you skip a day on your study plan, one of your chicks becomes a drumstick, so there's a real reason to keep your streak going."
